Who We Are

Located in Silala, Ganze Sub-County, Kilifi County, Kenya, George Allen School is dedicated to unlocking the potential of every child. We believe that education goes beyond the classroom by combining academic growth, character formation, and active community engagement.

Our Pillars of Excellence

  • Academic Brilliance: We provide strong foundational learning that prepares students for future educational success.
  • Community Empowerment: We work with local leaders, CBOs, and community initiatives to support positive social impact.
  • Character Development: We nurture integrity, responsibility, and leadership under our guiding motto: "The Sky Is The Limit".
